How Plugg works

From posting a campaign to a creator getting paid — here's every step, for both sides.

🎬 For Creators

Create your stats profile

Add your Instagram followers, YouTube subscribers, highest reel views, niche and engagement rate. This is your pitch.

Start your 30-day free trial

Browse and bid on unlimited campaigns free for 30 days. After that, continue on the Pro plan.

Bid on campaigns you love

Set your price and pitch why you're the right fit. Brands compare you against other creators.

Get approved → unlock the brief

Once a brand approves your bid, the full video concept and deliverables are revealed to you.

Deliver & get paid

Publish the content and receive a secure payout. Build your reputation for the next gig.

Start free as a creator

🏷️ For Brands

Post your product / campaign

Add a title, description, budget, deadline and deliverables. Keep the creative concept private for now.

Receive bids from creators

Creators bid their price and pitch. You see each one's reach, engagement and niche in a single view.

Compare & approve the best fit

Pick the creator who matches your audience and budget. Approving auto-declines the rest.

Share your video concept

Now reveal the full brief — concept, script direction and deliverables — to your chosen creator.

Pay securely on delivery

Funds flow through the payment gateway. You pay only a small platform commission on accepted bids.
