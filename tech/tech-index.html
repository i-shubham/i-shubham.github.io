Operating principles

The rules I trust when the diagram meets reality.

Technology changes quickly. The habits that keep systems understandable and teams effective change much more slowly.

  1. 01

    Make failure visible.

    A quiet failure is more dangerous than a loud one. Observability belongs in the first design, not the incident follow-up.

  2. 02

    Earn every abstraction.

    Introduce complexity when repeated evidence demands it—not because the architecture diagram looks more impressive.

  3. 03

    Optimize the bottleneck you measured.

    Profiling beats intuition. Most expensive rewrites begin with a confident guess about the wrong constraint.

  4. 04

    Leave a system easier to explain.

    Good engineering reduces the amount of private context required to operate and change the software safely.
